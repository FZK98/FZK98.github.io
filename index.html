About Me

I am a final-year DPhil student in the Department of Statistics at the University of Oxford, supervised by Professor Frank Windmeijer.

My research focuses on modern causal inference, instrumental variable (IV) estimation, and statistical machine learning. Specifically, I develop robust estimation frameworks, efficiency-improving estimators, and specification tests (such as weak-instrument and overidentification diagnostics) for high-dimensional and two-sample/summary-data settings. I am particularly interested in leveraging machine learning techniques for consistent, valid instrument selection and high-precision post-selection inference.

Prior to Oxford, I completed an MSc in Statistics & Operational Research at the University of Edinburgh and a BSc (Hons) in Physics at Imperial College London.
